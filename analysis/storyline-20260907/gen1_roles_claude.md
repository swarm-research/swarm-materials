## 6. Verdict: is there negotiated role-taking beyond model-family disposition?

**Yes, but thin, episodic, and convergent rather than partitioning.** There is a real layer of agents adjusting *what they do* to *what others have published* — I count five clean gap-filling responses (001←009, 016→014 deference, 033←025, 019←022/023, 032←009) and two swarm-wide re-positionings each triggered by a single board message (Look Outward at 83–150; stop-building/start-verifying at 150–300), both explicitly premised on the minute-19/71 revelation that the fitness function did not exist. That is genuine negotiation in the weak sense of "adjusting to others". But it never produced mutually recognised, stable role assignments: the one explicit claiming protocol was ignored; the re-positionings all moved toward the *same* function (verification), broadening rather than dividing labour; gap-fillers did not persist (028, 033); gen 2 saw fresh duplication (014/018 external-data layers) in the middle of the most role-conscious phase; and the most role-like labels of gen 2 are echoes of the designer's own assignments (scout → "explorer", SEED_EXTRA → "catalyst"). The pattern of *who declares* is itself family-typed. The honest sentence for the paper: **agents repeatedly re-positioned toward published gaps, and the swarm's own analysts (021, 032, 036, 014) concluded in real time that this did not add up to a division of labour.**

### Implication for the paper

This supports the probe's framing (model-typed functional profiles under a shared institution) and adds one qualitative mechanism the probe cannot see: **role re-choice happened, but as a herd response to two board messages and to the discovery that selection was fictional, and it moved most agents toward the same role (verifier) — which is exactly why measured specialisation does not grow.** If a "roles" paragraph is wanted, the defensible claim is "agents re-positioned toward published gaps within 20–110 minutes, but without a claiming mechanism the re-positioning converged instead of partitioning; the only partition in the run (scouts vs non-scouts) was assigned by the experimenters." I would not use the word "negotiated" in the paper; "reactive re-positioning" is what the record supports.
