# Does General Intelligence Specialize?

Pre-registration for ICLR 2027 submission.

**Full title**: Does General Intelligence Specialize? Emergent Role Differentiation, Adversarial Resilience, and Social Structure in Open-Ended Multi-Agent LLM Swarms

**Authors**: Xuanyi Wang (ByteDance)

**Pre-registered**: 2026-08-02

## Abstract

We study whether identically-prompted general-purpose LLM agents spontaneously develop functional specialization under competitive selection pressure. Using a 1000-agent swarm with citation-based survival, active elimination, and covert adversarial agents, we test whether competitive environments inevitably produce role differentiation — and whether this represents emergent efficiency rather than capability degradation.

Agents are not told that selection pressure exists. They are given a workspace and asked to do research; nothing in their prompt mentions survival, citations-as-currency, or elimination, and the reaper announces nothing. Whether they infer the rule from their peers' disappearance is itself a pre-registered question (H9). See Amendment 1 for the full rationale.

## Key Hypotheses

- **H1**: Agents will spontaneously specialize even with identical initial prompts
- **H2**: Stronger selection pressure deepens specialization
- **H3**: Citation networks self-organize into disciplinary clusters
- **H4**: Swarms develop emergent immune responses to adversarial agents
- **H5**: Information asymmetry accelerates differentiation
